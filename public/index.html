a 7' tall, 24' long wooden ski ramp and rail in our backyard near Cherryhill Mall, London Ontario.

bring your skis, your board, your helmet, or just come hang. we've got redbull, food, a firepit, a trampoline, and a bbq going.

if you send the rail you're getting merch and prizes.

2021
chapter 01 // the beginning

The Roots

growing up in toronto, me and my friends would build rails in the backyard with limited success. that same rail made it all the way to london.

2024
chapter 02 // 4 months planning. 4 weekends building.

The Build

over 4 months in the summer of 2024 i planned out the entire build. across 4 weekends from late september to mid november, my dad and i built the ramp over full days, costing upwards of $2000. couldn't have done it without his help, his time, and his tools.

JAN
chapter 03 // january 2025. it became real.

First Jams

after tremblant in 2025, i recruited a bunch of people to come out to railjam. the turnout was amazing. people were enjoying free drinks and burgers (on my dime) and most importantly, trying to ski the rail. people wanted another one and i was eager to host again.

SUM
chapter 04 // it never stops

All Season

during the summer of 2025 we made some major upgrades. a new second rail, a trampoline, and a fireplace.

2026
chapter 05 // a dream come true

Red Bull Snow Day

as winter 2025/26 was hitting, me and my roommate mason were already planning the next jam. i heard about a redbull sponsored climbing event and knew i couldn't fumble my chance. went in person and pitched railjam to the ambassador, ella. she agreed. we were officially redbull sponsored athletes. biggest turnout yet with over 60 people in attendance.
